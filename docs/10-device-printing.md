# Device And Printing Requirements

## Device Requirements

- register terminal identity
- persist known device fingerprints
- support trust, revoke, and last-seen tracking
- inventory scanners, printers, drawers, and payment terminals

## Printing Requirements

- render customer receipts consistently
- preserve printable outputs for support and audit
- support ESC/POS-oriented flows first
- support later printer-specific adapters behind a stable print contract

## Replacement Gate

Do not sell native replacement where device and print requirements are unproven.
